Exact invariant

N(v+p^k t)/p^k = N(v)/p^k + (2Qv)·t (mod p)

Nonzero gradient ensures precisely p² solutions for t, so a regular branch never dies. At p=2 the gradient is always zero mod 2, requiring singular analysis.

Independent illustrative checks matched direct counts modulo p² for p=2,3,5,7; odd-prime witness chains were constructed through p⁶.
