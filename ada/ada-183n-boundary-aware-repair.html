Selection uses the original failure only; holdout uses disjoint deterministic synthetic cases. Safety gate: original failure must improve over the incumbent, holdout misses may not exceed incumbent misses by the allowed tolerance, and false alarms may not exceed incumbent by more than two. Passing this finite gate is not a universal safety guarantee.
