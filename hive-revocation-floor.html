QG-068 · HIVE TRUST SNAPSHOTS · FAIL-CLOSED REVOCATION

One revocation.
Every agent accountable.

An agent's local snapshot is not sufficient just because its signatures verify. Each online agent must meet a revocation sequence floor pinned separately from the snapshot. Offline, stale, and invalid agents cannot authorize new operations.
