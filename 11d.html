Three invariants to preserve

Identity: each vertex retains its unique n-bit address. Adjacency: an edge joins addresses at Hamming distance one. Projection: moving the 2D view does not change either.

For dimension n: V = 2ⁿ; E = n·2ⁿ⁻¹; square faces = n(n−1)·2ⁿ⁻³ for n ≥ 2. The 11D graph has 22 facets of dimension ten.

11D is not a physics proof

Superstring models normally use 10 spacetime dimensions; M-theory is associated with 11. In this lab, 11D means 11 independent mathematical graph coordinates. The globule is a proposed QUANTA envelope, not a verified compactification, brane, particle, or natural object.
