Experiment parameters

Training uses seed families 1000–1999 and holdout uses 9000–9999, with separate deterministic simulated regimes. The candidate is selected using training only, then measured against the unchanged incumbent on paired holdout sequences.

Three independent promotion gates

Held-out comparison and safety vetoes

ContactIncumbent costCandidate costImprovementCandidate missesIncumbent missesDecision

A candidate is rejected if any holdout trial breaches the hard minimum recovery floor, if it has more ground-truth event misses than the incumbent in aggregate, or if it fails the required mean paired improvement. This is a bounded simulation, not proof of real-world safety or statistical significance.

Immutable trial ledger

Each evaluation records train seeds, chosen candidate, held-out seed IDs, paired metrics and the promotion decision. Re-running a public, fixed holdout can leak information through repeated experimentation; for consequential use, rotate and seal an independent final test set.
