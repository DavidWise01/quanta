# QUANTA — Canonical Book Plan: 32.5 Chapters

*David Lee Wise · Working editorial map · 7 November 2026*

**Length target:** 32 full chapters followed by an intentional **half-chapter, 32.5**. The fraction is a literary form, not a claim about fractional physical dimensions. The last half-chapter is a deliberate open door: a return to the box without an invented final answer.

**Editorial constraint:** Keep the one-box story intelligible to a reader without advanced mathematics. Let `.|.|.|.|.` recur as a five-mark/four-boundary wall-shadow motif, while clearly distinguishing suggestive metaphor from a defined tesseract projection. The reader's experiments and the ledger remain separate from physics claims. Commit each new chapter, puzzle, website section, lexicon changes, ledger, and changelog to GitHub as part of its turn.

## Act I — Learn to See (1–8)

1. Zero — the reference.
2. The Dot — a mark.
3. The Gap — what lies between.
4. The Boundary — passage and distinction.
5. The Box — containment.
6. The Loop — return.
7. The Vessel — held change.
8. The Path — trace before arrival.

## Act II — Learn to Read (9–12)

9. The Signal — the shared reading rule.
10. The Code — syntax before meaning.
11. The Grammar — nesting, sequence, identity.
12. The Transformation — a wall shadow changes.

## Act III — Learn to Remember and Respond (13–20)

13. The Witness — observation without possession.
14. The Record — memory without rewriting.
15. The Comparison — what is being compared.
16. The Difference — what changed, with scope.
17. The Choice — distinguish option from action.
18. The Action — distinguish attempt from result.
19. The Feedback — compare a prediction against observed evidence.
20. **The Lesson** — retain what can inform the next choice.

## Act IV — Learn to Test (21–26)

21. **The Question** — curiosity and testable hypotheses.
22. **The Experiment** — vary one condition and record what happens.
23. **The Failure** — preserve the smallest honest counterexample.
24. **The Invariant** — what survives a declared transformation.
25. **The Boundary Again** — find where a rule stops holding.
26. **The Repetition** — distinguish reproducibility from coincidence.

## Act V — Learn to Connect (27–30)

27. **The Neighbor** — two boxes, separate identities.
28. **The Exchange** — messages with receipts and provenance.
29. **The Weave** — many paths, local witnesses, no invented consensus.
30. **The Homecoming** — return from the mesh to one box.

## Act VI — Close the Shape, Leave the Door (31–32.5)

31. **The Shadow Revisited** — revisit `.|.|.|.|.` with everything learned.
32. **The One Box** — the whole reader's journey folded into a single, accessible scene.
32.5. **The Half-Step** — a shorter coda; one unresolved gap, one honest witness, one page left open.

All titles from 20 onward are **planned, not yet drafted**. Their progression may be refined in later turns without changing the 32.5-chapter length. Chapter 19 is drafted and committed; the first 18 chapters are likewise committed.
