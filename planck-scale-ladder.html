⚠ THE CORRECTION: 10⁻¹⁵ ≠ CHEMISTRY
10⁻¹⁵ m (femtometer) — Nuclear. The strong force (QCD) confines quarks into protons and neutrons. Carrier: gluons. Coupling constant: α_s ≈ 1 (strong coupling). Proton charge radius: 0.8414 ± 0.0019 fm (2019 PRad measurement).

10⁻¹⁰ m (ångström) — Chemistry. The electromagnetic force (Coulomb + exchange) binds electrons to nuclei, makes orbitals, makes bonds. Carrier: photons. Coupling constant: α ≈ 1/137 (weak coupling). C–C bond: 1.54 Å. C=C: 1.34 Å. O–H: 0.96 Å.

Gap between them: 5 orders of magnitude. Different force, different carrier, different coupling, different physics entirely. The strong force doesn't make molecules. The electromagnetic force doesn't confine quarks.

THE PLANCK FOAM QUESTION

Claim: At 10⁻³⁵ m, spacetime itself fluctuates — topology changes, virtual wormholes, "foam."
Argument FOR: Heisenberg + GR. ΔE·Δt ≥ ℏ/2 at Planck time gives ΔE ~ E_Planck ≈ 1.22 × 10¹⁹ GeV.
That energy in a Planck volume makes a black hole. The metric cannot be smooth.

Argument AGAINST (measured):
· Fermi LAT (2009): GRB 090510 photons from z=0.9 (7.3 Gly). No energy-dependent dispersion.
· Constraint: M_QG > 1.2 × E_Planck for linear dispersion (n=1 model DEAD).
· MAGIC (2020): Confirmed. Mrk 501 flare. Same result.
· Simplest foam models predict Δv/c ~ (E/E_P)^n · (L/l_P). Ruled out for n=1, constrained for n=2.

Status:
· Wheeler foam (1955 picture, bubbling topology) — simplest versions observationally dead.
· Structure at Planck scale — almost certain (dimensional analysis demands it).
· What that structure IS — unknown. Holographic principle says d.o.f. ∝ area, not volume.
· The desert (10⁻³⁵ to 10⁻¹⁸) means no experiment can probe between Planck and LHC.

PLAIN VERSION (the toddler corner)

Imagine a ruler that goes from REALLY REALLY tiny to your height.

At the very bottom (10⁻³⁵): things are so small that space itself might be bumpy, like the surface of a sponge. But when scientists looked really carefully at light from far-away explosions, the light traveled perfectly smooth. So the "sponge" idea is probably wrong. Something is there, but it's not a sponge.

In the middle (10⁻¹⁵): this is where the bits inside atoms live — protons and neutrons. They're held together by the STRONG force, like super-strong glue.

A bit higher (10⁻¹⁰): this is where atoms hold hands to make molecules. That's CHEMISTRY. It uses a different, weaker "glue" — the electromagnetic force. This is where water, sugar, DNA, and everything you can touch gets built.

The strong glue makes the pieces. The electromagnetic glue makes them hold hands. Different glue, different job.

Between the sponge and the glue? NOTHING. For 16 steps of the ruler, nothing organizes at all. That empty stretch is called the desert.
