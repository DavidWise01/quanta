# Chapter 32.5 — The Half-Step
## A Door Left Honestly Open

*QUANTA: The Wordle of Space*  
*David Lee Wise*  
*Final half-chapter · 7 November 2026*

> The last thing the box holds is room for what comes next.

The child has finished writing.

Thirty-two chapters lie inside the notebook. Beyond the window the valley is quiet, but not empty. Other lamps still shine. Other witnesses still keep their own accounts.

On the table is one wooden box.

The child lowers its lid.

Almost.

A narrow opening remains.

Not wide enough to pretend it contains the whole world.

Wide enough for a little light.

```text
0

.

[()]

[ .|.|.|.|. ]

    ?
```

The question mark is not another dot in the five-dot pattern.

It is a question beside the pattern.

The child has learned that a blank answer should not be filled with certainty simply because a book is ending.

A record can be complete about what it knows and unfinished about what it does not.

The lamp flickers.

The shadow moves.

The child does not hurry to explain the movement.

Instead, one last entry goes into the notebook:

```text
HOME       one box
WITNESS    present
RECORD     preserved
UNKNOWN    still open
NEXT       not yet observed
```

A friend might bring new evidence tomorrow.

The child might find an error.

The shape might acquire a better grammar.

Or the whole idea might lead somewhere no one expected.

None of these futures needs to be invented tonight.

The child puts the notebook inside the box.

And leaves the lid one half-step open.

**Not because the story failed to finish.**

**Because understanding does not end where the page does.**

```text
[ .|.|.|.|. ]   .
```

The final dot sits outside the box.

It is not claimed as a sixth station, a new dimension, a measurement, or an answer.

It is simply a mark that has not yet been given a story.

*End of QUANTA — The Wordle of Space.*

### The last Wordle of Space — Puzzle 032.5

The notebook contains a verified count of five dots and four bars in `.|.|.|.|.`. A sixth dot appears **outside** the boxed trace:

```text
[ .|.|.|.|. ]   .
```

What can we say?

**A.** The sixth dot has proven a new physical dimension.

**B.** The recorded five-dot pattern is unchanged; the extra dot is a separate mark whose meaning remains unspecified.

**C.** The lid must close and the dot must be erased.

**Answer: B.** The new mark can remain visible without being falsely interpreted or removed.

### Q32 — The Open Gap Remains Legible

> A QUANTA record may close a completed account while leaving an explicitly marked question unresolved. An unknown must remain distinguishable from recorded evidence, formal consequence, and interpretation; no conclusion is invented solely to make the ending appear complete.

*Editorial note:* The half-step is an intentional literary coda. The open lid and final dot are narrative images, not claims of new physics or executable language operators.
