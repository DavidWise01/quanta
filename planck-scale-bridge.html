Three ways to say where and when you are:


Your calendar says the date (like November 17, 2026).
Your clock says the time (like 2:30 in the afternoon).
Your angle says which way you’re pointing
(like facing east, which is 90 degrees).


This page puts all three together in one address, like a full mailing
address but for time and direction.


The really tiny stuff at the bottom of the ladder? That’s where atoms live.
And even tinier than atoms, there’s a size called the “Planck length”
that’s SO small that scientists can’t measure anything smaller. We know the
size exists because the math demands it, but we can’t see what’s there.


The {{pb}} thing is a name tag for that tiny scale. It doesn’t
pretend to know what’s there — it just marks the spot.
