All-exponent theorem

For each canonical family:
Q(u,t) = q_F(u,ray·t+offset) + 2
Q(u,t) = 2 R(u,t),   R ∈ Z[u,t]

R has at least one nonsingular root mod 2.

By two-coordinate Hensel lifting:
  Q(u,t) ≡ 0 (mod 2^k)
  has solutions for every k ≥ 1.

Let r ∈ {1,2} be number of regular
R-roots modulo 2. For k ≥ 2:

    C_Q(k) ≥ r × 2^k.

These are LOWER BOUNDS, not general
exact counts; singular branches can add.
