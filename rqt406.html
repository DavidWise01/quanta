The Three Surviving Gates

The genuine inhomogeneous norm −2 problem has only three possible primitive parity families. All three admit exact two-adic constructions, but an integer root19 is not yet known.

Infinite local construction

Fix (y,z)=(0,1),(1,1),(1,2). Each quadratic in x has odd leading coefficient and discriminant 1 mod 8, hence a square root in the 2-adic integers. That yields solutions in each parity class at every power of two.
