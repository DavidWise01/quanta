Proof beyond finite enumeration

Every entry of Q is even. Put A=Q/2, fix y=0,z=1, and let a=A00, b=A02, c=A22+1. Since a and b are odd and 8 divides c, D=b²−ac is congruent to 1 modulo 8. D has a square root in the two-adic integers and a is invertible there. Hence x=(-b+sqrt(D))/a gives a two-adic solution.

This does not prove an ordinary integral root19. The position of the provisional root18 remains conditional.
